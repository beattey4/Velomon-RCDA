About Investment Fees
Most managed investment accounts are subject to annual advisory fees and/or commissions of 1–3% of the account balance. Enter the advisor fee here for cost-of-advice reporting — a 1.5% annual fee on a $500,000 account costs over $150,000 in the first 20 years alone, before accounting for foregone growth on those dollars.

Fees on pre-tax accounts (Traditional IRA / 401k) carry an additional hidden cost: because the balance was never taxed, each dollar lost to fees represents more than one dollar of real after-tax wealth. The analysis accounts for this using a tax-adjusted fee multiplier.
